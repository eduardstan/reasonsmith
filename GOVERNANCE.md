# Governance and contributor compensation

This page states plainly how reasonsmith is run, who decides what, and — because it matters and is usually left vague — the honest position on money.

## Who decides

reasonsmith is maintained by Ionel Eduard Stan (University of Milano-Bicocca). Final decisions on scope, merges, releases, and the project's direction rest with the maintainer.

Two rules constrain those decisions and are not negotiable:

- **No verdict is reported that the evidence does not support.** A duty that cannot be discharged honestly is reported as unattainable, not evaluated, or not applicable — never as satisfied. This is the reason the project exists.
- **Legal readings are recorded as assumptions, not asserted as fact.** Whether a formalisation faithfully renders a statutory duty is a legal question. Each one is written down, cited, and open to challenge in `docs/refinement.md`, rather than settled silently in code.

A contribution that weakens either rule will not be merged, however good the engineering.

## Money: the honest position

**reasonsmith is unfunded. Nobody is paid to work on it, including the maintainer, whose salary comes from the university and is not for this project.**

This is stated explicitly because the alternative — leaving it vague — lets contributors assume payment is coming and feel misled later. It is not coming today.

**What a contributor gets today.** Not money, but not nothing, and these are real:

- **Authorship and credit that can be verified.** Contributors are named in `CITATION.cff`, in release notes, and in the commit record. Substantial contributions are credited by name in the documentation they affect.
- **Co-authorship on academic output** where the contribution meets normal authorship standards. Several parts of this project are research contributions and will be published as such.
- **Maintainer roles** for sustained contributors, with real authority over the areas they maintain.
- **A public, citable record** of work in formal methods and AI regulatory conformance — which is the currency that matters for academic and industry positions.

**If funding arrives.** The maintainer is actively pursuing research grants and other funding. If money arrives:

- it will be stated openly here — the amount, the source, and its constraints;
- contributors who did the work will be paid from it where the funding instrument permits paying non-employees, and the maintainer will not treat "the rules made it awkward" as a reason not to try;
- allocation will be discussed with the contributors concerned before it is decided, not announced afterwards.

**What is not promised.** No equity, no revenue share, no guaranteed payment, and no timeline. Any of the above may fail. Contribute because the work is worth doing and the credit is real — not on the expectation of income.

## Contributing

`CONTRIBUTING.md` has the development setup, the verification commands, and the standing rules a change must respect. Open issues describe the work that is actually wanted, each with a statement of what "done" looks like.

Questions go to Discussions. Conduct expectations are in `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.
